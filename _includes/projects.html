LLM Mathematical Reasoning Capstone

  • PyTorch, Hugging Face TRL, GRPO, Kubernetes

Improved a fixed 4B-parameter LLM's (Qwen3-4B) held-out math reasoning accuracy from 0.407 to 0.581, a 43% relative gain, under a no-additional-compute constraint. Ran around 40 version-controlled experiments across SFT, LoRA/QLoRA, and GRPO reinforcement learning, diagnosing and fixing output truncation as the primary failure mode. Trained on UCSD's DSMLP Kubernetes cluster with checkpoint-to-Hub resumption across a 12-hour container ceiling, and used vLLM continuous batching to cut evaluation time from around 93 hours to 2 to 4 hours.
